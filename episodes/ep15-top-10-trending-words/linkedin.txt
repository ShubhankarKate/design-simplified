143,199 posts in one second. How does an app find the top 10 trending words without counting every one?

Top-K heavy hitters is a common system design interview question. The compact answer:
- A Count-Min sketch: 5 rows of 2,719 counters, about 54 KB in total
- Each word hashes to one counter per row and adds 1
- A word's count is the smallest of its 5 counters, so it can overcount but never undercount
- A heap keeps the current top 10

Your trending list is a very good estimate, not an exact count.
