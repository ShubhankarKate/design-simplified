The sale starts at midnight. Why did your alert show up 17 minutes late?

Design Simplified · 12: sending 1 crore push alerts before a sale, a classic system design interview round.

- The naive answer: loop over every user at 12:00 and call send. That's where the round goes wrong.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging's default quota is 6 lakh messages a minute, so 1 crore alerts take about 17 minutes. Burst past it and you get 429 errors.
- Step 1: split users into batches of 500 (the most one call can take), so 20,000 jobs go into a queue.
- Step 2: workers pace to the quota, 10,000 a second, steady instead of spiky.
- Step 3: set a time to live. Without one, an offline phone can get "sale is live" up to 4 weeks later.
- The twist: FCM's own docs say its traffic more than doubles in the first 30 seconds to 2 minutes of each hour. So start about 17 minutes early (11:41) and finish by 11:58, clear of the rush.
