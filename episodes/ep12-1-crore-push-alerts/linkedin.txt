The sale starts at midnight. Your alert arrives 17 minutes late. Why?

Sending 1 crore push alerts is a classic system design interview round, and the naive answer (loop over every user at 12:00 and call send) is where it goes wrong.

- FCM's default quota is 6 lakh messages a minute, so 1 crore alerts take about 17 minutes
- Batch users 500 at a time: 20,000 jobs in a queue
- Workers pace to the quota, 10,000 a second, steady instead of spiky
- Set a time to live, or an offline phone can get "sale is live" weeks later
- FCM traffic more than doubles at the top of each hour, so start at 11:41 and finish by 11:58

Your call: a phone is offline till 12:30. Send the alert late, or drop it? A or B?
