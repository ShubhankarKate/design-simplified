Your live score froze in the last over. 6 to win. Why?

One expired cache key.

Every phone reads the score from the same key. When it expires, every request misses at the same moment and goes to the database. At 10,000 requests a second and a 200 ms refill, that's about 2,000 copies of one query.

How Facebook handled it (Scaling Memcache, NSDI 2013):
- The first miss gets a lease and is the only one allowed to refill the key
- Everyone else waits a few milliseconds and reads the fresh value
- Peak database queries for hot keys fell from 17,000 a second to 1,300
