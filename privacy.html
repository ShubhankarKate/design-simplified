Privacy policy

Design Simplified posting · last updated 20 October 2026

What this app is

"Design Simplified posting" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Design Simplified YouTube channel (@design_simplified_ai) and Instagram account (@design_simplified.ai) to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload to upload videos to the owner's own channel, and the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram account. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared or sold. The tool keeps a local record of which videos were posted and their links. Nothing else is stored.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access) or Meta's Business Integrations settings.

Use of Google data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
